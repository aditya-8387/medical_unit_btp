Welcome to the Institute Medical Center

The Institute Medical Center is dedicated to ensuring the health and well-being of our entire student and staff community. Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality, and compassionate healthcare services right here on campus. We believe that a healthy body and mind are essential for academic and personal success.

Our team of qualified medical professionals is equipped to handle routine check-ups, first aid, emergency care, and provide guidance on a healthy lifestyle. We maintain a well-stocked inventory of essential medicines and are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and privacy of all patient records.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence for campus healthcare, promoting a culture of wellness and ensuring that every member of the institute has the support they need to thrive.
